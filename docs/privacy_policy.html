DocScan Arab — Privacy Policy

✅ Zero-Data App

Last updated: July 12, 2026

TL;DR

DocScan Arab does not collect any data. Period.
All processing is done 100% offline on your device. No data ever leaves your phone.

Data We Collect

None. DocScan Arab collects:

  • ❌ No account information
  • ❌ No personal data
  • ❌ No usage statistics
  • ❌ No crash reports
  • ❌ No analytics
  • ❌ No advertising IDs
  • ❌ No location data
  • ❌ No camera feed (except during active scan)
  • ❌ No cloud storage
  • ❌ No third-party SDKs that collect data

How It Works

  1. You take a photo of a document
  2. The image is processed on your device using Tesseract OCR
  3. Extracted text is displayed and optionally saved locally
  4. All data remains on your device — no network requests are made

Permissions

  • Camera: Only used when you actively scan a document. Never accessed in background.
  • Storage: Only used to save scanned documents locally. You control what stays.

Internet Access

DocScan Arab does not require internet access. The app functions fully offline.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date. You will be notified in-app.
